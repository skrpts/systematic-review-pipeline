## Capability

Builds the extraction instrument a systematic review needs: a structured form whose fields follow
from the review's own research questions, rather than a generic template. Then applies it to each
included study so the resulting rows are comparable.

## What It Does

- derives extraction fields from the review's research questions
- specifies, per field, what counts as an acceptable value
- records where a study does not report a field, rather than leaving it blank
- keeps the form consistent across studies so rows can be compared

## When to Use

- after screening has settled which studies are included
- before synthesis, which depends on comparable extracted data

## Inputs

The review's research questions and the set of included studies.

## Outputs

A completed extraction form per included study, with unreported fields marked as such.
