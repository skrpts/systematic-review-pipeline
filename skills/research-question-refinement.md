## Capability

Turns a draft research question into one a systematic search can actually answer. Applies PICO
(Population, Intervention, Comparator, Outcome) for quantitative reviews or SPIDER (Sample,
Phenomenon of Interest, Design, Evaluation, Research type) for qualitative and mixed-methods work,
and states the review's objectives and scope alongside it.

## What It Does

- structures the question into the components the chosen framework names
- sets the review's objectives and scope explicitly
- judges whether the question is answerable by a literature search, and says which way it is wrong
  when it is not — too broad to be tractable, or too narrow to find studies
- flags where an existing review may already answer it

## When to Use

- at the very start of a review, before any search strategy is written
- whenever a question has been drafted but not yet made searchable

## Inputs

The draft research question, the field of study, and the intended review scope.

## Outputs

A refined, structured research question with stated objectives and scope.
